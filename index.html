Our Story

20 Years of Premium Pet Care

At Diamond Lux Pet Spa, we believe that grooming is more than just a haircut—it’s an essential part of your pet’s overall health, happiness, and well-being.

Deep Roots in Jacksonville

For over two decades, Diamond Lux Pet Spa has been a proud, trusted fixture in the Jacksonville, Florida community. What started 20 years ago as a family-run passion project has blossomed into the city's premier destination for luxury pet styling.

We have spent the last 20 years perfecting our craft, building lifelong relationships with our clients, and watching generations of pets grow up in our care. Being based right here on San Jose Blvd means we intimately understand the unique needs of Florida pets—from managing heavy shedding during our warmer months to protecting sensitive skin from the elements.

Our long-standing history is a testament to the absolute trust our community places in us every single day.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to provide an elite, premium grooming experience that doesn't compromise on competitive market value. We strive to set the gold standard in the pet care industry by combining high-end luxury with genuine, compassionate care that puts your pet's physical and mental health first.

Holistic Health First

Every bath, brush, and trim is meticulously designed to support your pet's dermatological and physical well-being. We exclusively use top-tier, pet-safe, and nourishing products.

Stress-Free Environment

We know grooming can be scary. We maintain a calm, soothing, and unhurried atmosphere so your furry family members feel safe, loved, and relaxed throughout their entire spa day.

Unmatched Value

With two decades of expertise, our styling and safety standards are unmatched. We are proud to offer competitive pricing and aggressive marketing value for a truly luxurious, high-end service.
